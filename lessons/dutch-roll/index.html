About the model

Linear lateral-directional equations (β, p, r, φ) integrated with a fixed-step RK4 method at 120 Hz. Yaw damper: rudder = −K × washed-out yaw rate (washout τ = 4 s), with actuator lag and rate and position limits. The purple arrow at the nose is the flight path; its angle to the nose is the sideslip β (true size).

A320 geometry is from Airbus documents. The stability derivatives and inertias are typical values for this class of aircraft, approximate and for teaching. All values and sources are in src/config/dutchRoll.ts.
